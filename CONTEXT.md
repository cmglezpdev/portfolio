# Portfolio

The public portfolio presents Carlos Manuel González's professional work and selected projects.

## Language

**Professional experience**:
Work performed in a professional role, described through responsibilities, contributions, and outcomes.

**Project**:
A specific product or technical initiative that can be described independently of an employment role. Its source code may be public or private.

**Selected work**:
A curated selection of projects presented to help visitors assess Carlos's work.

**Case study**:
A public account of a project's problem, Carlos's contribution, technical decisions, and outcome. It distinguishes delivered capabilities from future ideas.

**Capability tag**:
A concise label connecting a project's implemented work to an engineering skill or technology. Planned features do not qualify as current capabilities.

**Writing**:
Articles authored by Carlos, potentially published on the portfolio or external platforms. Writing is outside the first release.

**Professional project**:
A project delivered within paid employment. Public descriptions must distinguish Carlos's own contributions from existing functionality and team work.

**Personal project**:
A project Carlos builds outside his employment, often for his own use. It may be useful evidence of engineering work without having a large audience.
